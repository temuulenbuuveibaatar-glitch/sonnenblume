Dose table (flight surgeon)

Doses the dose check is allowed to calculate. Only two over-the-counter painkillers come pre-filled, from standard adult labels; every other dose must be set by the flight surgeon.

Shelf lives here are illustrative for the demo. A 2024 study of the ISS formulary found most medicines would expire before a roughly three-year Mars mission ends, and their stability in space is not well known. Real kits follow the flight surgeon's inventory.
