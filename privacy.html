Privacy Charter

Your body, your data. It stays with you.

Sonnenblume handles some of the most personal information there is: heart rate, mood, sleep and symptoms. This charter explains exactly what happens to it, in plain language.

On your device only

Every reading is saved in your browser's local storage. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party fonts or scripts.

You are in control

Export everything in one tap, or delete everything at the bottom of this page.

What our servers see

  • Hosting (Vercel). Like any website, the host receives standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not add any tracking to it.
  • Medical library (Microsoft Azure Cosmos DB). When connected, the Library tab reads medications, protocols and shielding data from Azure. These requests ask for reference information only. They never include your readings, name or anything you typed.

Principles for crew health data

  • Medical confidentiality. In real missions, a crew member's medical data is shared only with the flight surgeon and the people they choose, as in private medical conferences. Sonnenblume follows the same idea: nothing leaves the device unless the crew member exports it.
  • Minimum data. We only ask for what is needed to judge health status. There are no free-text diaries and no location tracking.
  • Crew alerts are deliberate. The automatic alert to the crew leader and medical officer exists for safety when 2 or more indicators are at Act now. It is described up front so nobody is surprised by it.
  • No secondary use. Data is never sold, used for advertising, or used to judge fitness for duty.

Security

  • The site is served over HTTPS with a strict Content Security Policy that blocks outside scripts.
  • All stored data is validated every time it is loaded, so corrupted or tampered data cannot run code in the page.
  • Anyone with access to your unlocked device and browser can see your data. On a shared device, delete your data when you are finished.

Your controls

Export: open the console, go to Log measurements and choose Export data.

Delete: this removes every reading, message, setting and the offline copy from this browser. It cannot be undone.

Not a medical device

Sonnenblume is a student prototype for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. In an emergency, follow your crew medical checklist and contact your medical team.
